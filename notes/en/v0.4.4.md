Arabic and accented searches are fixed on Windows; buttons now have tooltips.

## New
- **Button tooltips:** Hover over an icon button for a moment to see what it does.
- **Clearer shuffle:** Shuffling now tells you how many songs were shuffled, and a dot under the button shows it's on. A second click restores the original order, which is now announced too. Repeat shows the same dot.
- **Installer language:** The Turkish installer shows the license in Turkish. Portuguese (Brazil) was added to the installer.

## Fixes
- **Search (Windows):** Searches in Arabic, or with letters like ş, ğ or ı, were sent as garbled text and returned unrelated playlists. Fixed.
- **Halo colors:** In "From cover" mode some covers produced rainbow colors that aren't on the cover. The glow now stays in the cover's own colors.
- **Accent-color Halo:** The glow fades more softly, stays balanced with very dark or very light accents, and changes color smoothly.
- **Volume:** If Vynz was closed unexpectedly (e.g. from Task Manager), the volume was forgotten. It's now also saved while playing.
