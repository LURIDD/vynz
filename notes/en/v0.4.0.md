Vynz now speaks English.

## New
- **English interface:** The whole interface, notifications and release notes are now available in English. Vynz follows your system language: Turkish if your computer is set to Turkish, English otherwise.
- **Language setting:** Choose System, Türkçe or English under Settings → Appearance → Language; the change applies immediately.
- **Regional content:** With the English interface, Home trends and recommendations follow your computer's region instead of Turkey.
