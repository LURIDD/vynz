The Halo glow is now more vivid and adjustable.

## New
- **Halo color:** With Halo selected under Appearance → Background glow, the light can take its color from the cover or from the accent color.
- **Halo spread:** A new slider controls how far the light spreads.

## Fixes
- **Faint halo:** On black, gray or dark covers the halo faded into the background. It now leans toward the cover's most vivid color.
- **Missing halo:** For some songs whose cover had already been loaded in a list, the halo wasn't drawn at all. Fixed.
